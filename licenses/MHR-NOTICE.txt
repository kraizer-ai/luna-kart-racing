Modifications (Luna Kart Racing, November 2026)
-----------------------------------------------
karts/body/driver.glb is not the MHR model: it is a new file made from MHR's output and data by this project's
bake tools (tools/kart/body in the game's sources). What was changed:

  - The body was given this game's body types with MHR's own shape and skeleton parameters (a default slim build, a
    woman's shape, five heights, five builds), and the upper arm of the mean skeleton was lengthened.
  - It was posed seated in this game's kart, the hands closed on the steering wheel's rim, at the key poses the game
    needs (driving straight, full lock both ways, a hand on the gear lever, on the handbrake, at the visor, the
    fingers open). The mesh is stored in the straight-ahead driving pose with MHR's pose correctives in the shape.
  - The differences between plain skinning and MHR's output at the other key poses, and the shape differences of
    the body types, were stored as morph targets. MHR's corrective network itself is not part of the game.
  - The skin where it touches the rim was laid on the rim; the surface was smoothed and moved outwards as clothing
    (a racing suit, gloves); the head above the neck and the feet were removed (the game's own helmet and boots are
    worn there) and the neck was closed.
  - Lighter levels of detail were made by removing triangles; joints that move no vertex were removed; attributes
    were quantised; the joint transforms of the key poses were tabulated for each skeleton.
  - The suit's and the gloves' design (stored as distance fields in MHR's texture layout) and its normal map are
    this project's own work.

MHR's code, its corrective network and its other assets are not distributed with this game.
